## 📖 Overview

This course examines the legal and ethical implications of data science, artificial intelligence, and big data analytics. Topics covered include:

- **Data Protection:** GDPR compliance, privacy by design, data governance
- **AI Ethics:** Bias, fairness, transparency, accountability
- **Automated Decision Making:** Profiling, scoring, predictions
- **Fundamental Rights:** Privacy, freedom of expression, non-discrimination
- **Sector-Specific Issues:** Healthcare, public administration, workplace, education

## 🎯 Assignment Topics

Available research topics include:

1. **Data Protection by Design** - Implications for data management
2. **Profiling and Data Protection** - Legal frameworks and limitations
3. **IoT and Data Protection** - Privacy challenges in connected devices
4. **AI and GDPR** - Personal data processing with AI systems
5. **Scoring/Predictions** - Use of personal data for automated assessments
6. **Face Recognition Systems** - Uses and legal issues
7. **Predictive Policing** - AI in law enforcement
8. **Emotion Recognition** - Workplace and education applications
9. **Human Oversight of AI** - Accountability and control mechanisms
10. **Automated Decision Making** - Legal requirements and safeguards
11. **AI in Healthcare** - Medical AI applications and regulations
12. **AI in Public Sector** - Government use of AI systems
13. **Generative AI Legal Issues** - Copyright, liability, authenticity
14. **AI and Fundamental Rights** - Impact on democracy and rights
15. **Data Governance Act** - EU data sharing framework
16. **Free Speech vs Fake News** - Disinformation detection
17. **Hate Speech Detection** - Balancing free expression
18. **LLMs and Misinformation** - Large language model challenges

## 📚 Key Concepts

### GDPR Principles
- Lawfulness, fairness, transparency
- Purpose limitation
- Data minimization
- Accuracy
- Storage limitation
- Integrity and confidentiality
- Accountability

### AI Ethics Frameworks
- Transparency and explainability
- Fairness and non-discrimination
- Human oversight and control
- Privacy and data protection
- Accountability and liability

### EU AI Act
- Risk-based approach to AI regulation
- Prohibited AI practices
- High-risk AI systems requirements
- Transparency obligations
